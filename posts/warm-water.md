For eleven years I did not go anywhere warm on purpose. I had a whole position about it, and the position was that ease is the enemy of attention — that a place which hands you a good time has no reason to show you anything else, and you have no reason to look.

I still think there's something in that. I have written entire essays out of being cold, bored and slightly angry in northern harbours, and I have never once written anything out of being comfortable.^[The nearest I came was a paragraph in Palermo about a very good sandwich, which I deleted, because the sandwich was doing all the work and I was just nearby.]

What I had not accounted for is that the position had stopped being a position and become a habit, and habits do not know why they exist.

## The forty minutes

Six in the morning, because I cannot sleep anywhere. Nobody on the sand. Water at the temperature where you do not notice entering it, which I had always held against it — there is no threshold, no decision, nothing to be brave about.

I swam out past where it mattered and turned around and floated.

::: pull
It turns out you can pay attention to a pleasant thing. I had simply never tried, because I had decided in advance what it would be worth.
:::

And what I noticed, floating there being handed a good time, was that the boats went out in a specific order, and that the order was not about size or speed, and that a man on the third one shouted something at a man on the fifth one every morning for four mornings, and that on the fourth morning the fifth boat shouted back and both of them laughed.

Which is exactly the kind of thing I have flown to cold places to find.

::: aside
Eleven years. He would like you to know that he is aware this is the whole essay, and that it is not a flattering one, and that he has told it to almost nobody in person.
:::

## What the prejudice was actually doing

Not protecting my attention. Protecting my *self-image* — the part that would rather be the man who goes to Reykjavík in February than the man on a beach, because one of those is a personality and the other is a holiday.

That is vanity wearing the costume of rigour, and it cost me eleven years of a perfectly good hemisphere.

> A place does not owe you difficulty for its insights to count.

I have not become a person who lies by a pool. The water was at body temperature and I was still up at six, and I still cannot sleep, and I still took notes. But I have retired the position, and I would like it on record that it was retired for cause, not because I got tired.

Four mornings. The fifth boat shouted back. I would have missed it on principle.
